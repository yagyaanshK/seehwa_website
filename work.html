Our work

Focus areas for resilient mountain systems.

Seven connected focus areas, each addressing a different pressure on high-altitude life — and each designed to reinforce the others.

01 Sustainability & Climate Resilience 02 Energy & HVAC 03 Ecology 04 Water & Waste Management 05 Agri-Ecology 06 Health 07 Livelihood & Rural Development

01 — Sustainability & Climate Resilience

Planning mountain communities for net-zero, circular and adaptive futures.

SEEHWA combines sustainability and climate resilience because fragile high-altitude regions need mitigation and adaptation at the same time. The work centres on net-zero emissions, resource circularity, low-impact design and community resilience planning.

  • Climate-sensitive settlement and infrastructure planning.
  • Net-zero and circular economy models for remote mountain communities.
  • Community resilience planning for water stress, harsh winters and shifting livelihoods.
  • Applied research and advisory inputs for climate-responsive development.

02 — Energy & HVAC

Clean energy and low-energy comfort systems for extreme cold.

High-altitude reliability depends on both clean power and efficient thermal comfort. The work covers renewable systems, passive solar buildings, decentralized microgrids and low-energy heating and ventilation suited to cold, remote regions.

  • Solar and renewable energy planning for off-grid and semi-grid settlements.
  • Passive solar design, building energy efficiency and cold-climate retrofits.
  • Decentralized microgrid concepts for community infrastructure.
  • Low-energy heating and ventilation for homes, institutions and community spaces.
  • Technical advisory linking health, comfort, energy demand and livability.

03 — Ecology

Regenerating fragile ecosystems through nature-based solutions.

Ecological stability in mountain regions directly affects water, agriculture, settlement safety, health and livelihoods. Our ecology work focuses on regeneration, biodiversity conservation and nature-based solutions.

  • Ecological restoration and regeneration planning.
  • Biodiversity-sensitive project design and conservation support.
  • Watershed, soil and land-use sensitivity inputs.
  • Nature-based solutions for climate and development challenges.

04 — Water & Waste Management

Circular systems for water, sanitation and waste.

We work on circular water systems, greywater reuse, sanitation and zero-waste solutions — systems that reduce pressure on fragile ecosystems while improving basic services and local quality of life.

  • Greywater reuse, sanitation and decentralized treatment systems.
  • Solid waste reduction, segregation and zero-waste planning.
  • Water security approaches suited to cold, dry mountain settlements.
  • Technical support for institutions, community facilities and rural infrastructure.

05 — Agri-Ecology

Climate-smart agriculture rooted in regenerative practice.

Agri-ecology is the bridge between food security, soil health, traditional knowledge, livelihood resilience and ecological regeneration. We support climate-smart agriculture, organic farming, composting and regenerative practice.

  • Organic and low-input farming practices for cold-arid conditions.
  • Composting and circular organic resource reuse.
  • Support for regenerative agriculture and soil health.
  • Integration of local agricultural knowledge with climate-smart methods.

06 — Health

Strengthening preventive care and last-mile health access.

Health work in remote high-altitude settlements is connected to water, sanitation, housing, mobility, climate stress and local institutional capacity. We work on community-based access, preventive care and last-mile infrastructure.

  • Community health access and preventive care initiatives.
  • Health camps, awareness and local screening support.
  • Data-informed understanding of last-mile health needs.
  • Healthcare infrastructure support for remote settlements.

07 — Livelihood & Rural Development

Building infrastructure, education access and income pathways together.

We approach livelihood and rural development as one integrated system: strengthening physical infrastructure, expanding access to education, and promoting income generation for sustainable livelihoods and holistic wellbeing.

  • Integrated village and rural development planning.
  • Infrastructure support connected to wellbeing, mobility and access.
  • Education access and local capacity-building pathways.
  • Income generation linked to ecological, cultural and local strengths.
