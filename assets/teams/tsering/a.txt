Tsering Dolma
Visual Designer & Illustrator

About — I work as a Graphic Designer and Illustrator at Seehwa Green Foundation, where I use creativity and storytelling to support work that connects people, communities, and the environment. I hold a Bachelor of Arts from the University of Delhi and am a self-taught artist who has been drawn to art and nature since childhood. Growing up in Ladakh has deeply shaped the way I see and create, inspiring me to explore the relationship between culture, people, and the natural world. Through my work at Seehwa Green Foundation, I hope to contribute creatively to meaningful initiatives that nurture communities and encourage a deeper connection with our environment.
