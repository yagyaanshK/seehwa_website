Core team

The people doing the work.

Ar. Shruti Ajabe

Sustainable Design Executive

I am an architect and Sustainable Design Executive at Seehwa Green, holding a B.Arch from BNCA, Pune. With professional experience across Pune, Dehradun, and Ladakh, I have worked in landscape, residential, and commercial architecture, along with on-site execution.

Currently, I focus on sustainable and climate-responsive architecture in Ladakh, striving to create resilient, resource-conscious, and context-sensitive structures that respond to the region’s unique climate, materials, culture, and communities.

Tsering Dolma

Visual Designer & Illustrator

I work as a Graphic Designer and Illustrator at Seehwa Green Foundation, where I use creativity and storytelling to support work that connects people, communities, and the environment. I hold a Bachelor of Arts from the University of Delhi and am a self-taught artist who has been drawn to art and nature since childhood.

Growing up in Ladakh has deeply shaped the way I see and create, inspiring me to explore the relationship between culture, people, and the natural world. Through my work at Seehwa Green Foundation, I hope to contribute creatively to meaningful initiatives that nurture communities and encourage a deeper connection with our environment.
