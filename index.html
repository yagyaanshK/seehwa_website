Who we are

Ladakh is warming faster than the plains. We build what comes next.

SEEHWA Green Foundation is a Section 8 non-profit headquartered in Leh. We design and deliver practical systems for cold, remote, high-altitude communities — the places where conventional development models simply do not work.

Our work spans renewable energy and cold-climate HVAC, circular water and waste systems, ecosystem regeneration, climate-smart agriculture, last-mile healthcare and rural livelihoods. We treat these as one connected system, because in a mountain village they are.
